About ConnectWork

ConnectWork is a two-sided platform connecting freelancers with clients who need work done. Clients post jobs describing what they need and a budget; freelancers can search by keyword and category, review job requirements, and apply with a message.

Once a freelancer applies, the client can review their application and either accept or decline it. Both sides can track status in real time from their own dashboard — pending, accepted, or declined.

This version runs entirely in your browser using local storage, so it's a fast way to demonstrate the full flow — registering as either role, posting jobs, applying, and managing applications — without needing a real backend or database.

Profile details, account information, job listings, applications, and contact messages stay in this browser. This frontend demo does not provide server-side authentication, send email, or synchronize data between devices.
